Help

In four steps

  1. Add photosDrop them on the left panel, click the box, or paste with Ctrl+V. Up to 12.
  2. Pick a layoutAuto layout reads the photo shapes. Or choose a variant, or shuffle.
  3. Fine-tuneClick a photo, then drag inside the frame to move it and scroll to zoom.
  4. ExportDownload as JPG, PNG or WEBP, copy to the clipboard, or print.

Good to know

  • Drag a thumbnail onto a frame to place or swap that photo.
  • Drag thumbnails past each other to change the order.
  • Double-click a frame to switch between Fill and Fit.
  • Looks apply one preset; "Apply to all" copies it to every photo.
  • Save project keeps photos and settings in one file for later.
  • Nothing is uploaded — the browser does all the work.
